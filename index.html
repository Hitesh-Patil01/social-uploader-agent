Social Uploader

Social Uploader is open-source software. It lets a person upload and schedule his or her own videos on their own YouTube channel, Facebook Pages and Instagram accounts, and move video files from a folder in their own Google Drive to the computer that runs the tool. Whoever uses the published code runs their own copy, with their own accounts and their own Google project.
